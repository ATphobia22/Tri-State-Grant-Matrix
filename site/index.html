Application document checklist

A planning checklist, not a claim that all items are mandatory. Choose a specific grant in the document generator for its project-related checklist.

Entity & governance

Governing-body authorizing resolution, organization legal name and structure, authorized representative, UEI and active SAM.gov registration when required, state registrations where required, nonprofit determination letter if relevant.

Financial & matching

Detailed costs and quantities, budget narrative, documented matching commitment, finance statements, indirect cost agreement or de minimis rate when allowed, procurement plan, prevailing wage and BABA documentation if applicable.

Project & engineering

Scope, service-area map, ownership and easements, benefit population, schedule, risk and alternatives, Preliminary Engineering Report where required, environmental and historic review, FEMA BCA when applicable, public notices and supporting exhibits.
